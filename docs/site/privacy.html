Oina Games

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what the Oina Games app (“the app”, “we”) does with information. It applies to the iOS and Android versions. The app is a collection of offline puzzle and arcade games. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not show third-party ads inside the app.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, high scores, favourites, recently played games, language, theme and sound or vibration settings are stored locally on your device. They never leave it, and removing the app deletes them.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It records usage events such as which game was opened, when a round started or ended, the score and round length, and settings changes such as the chosen language or theme. It also receives technical information provided by the platform, for example the device model, operating system version, app version, country or region derived from the network connection, and an anonymous app-instance identifier. We do not send what you type, and we do not link this data to your identity.

Google processes this data under its own terms: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

App ratings

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it using Apple’s or Google’s built-in rating prompt. Your answer is handled by the App Store or Google Play and is not shared with us beyond what the store publishes.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The analytics described above is anonymous and not used for advertising.

Your choices

  • You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app.
  • The app does not use your advertising ID on Android or iOS.
  • To ask about the data associated with your device, contact us using the address below.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
